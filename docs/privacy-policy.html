Privacy Policy — HomeKept

Last updated: 2 October 2026

HomeKept ("the app", package com.ahmadessam.homekept) is developed by Ahmad Essam. This policy explains exactly what the app handles.

Your home data never leaves your phone. Your home profile, your task schedule, your own custom tasks, your completion history, every cost you record, and the appliances, warranty dates and photos in your vault are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared.

You do not need an account to use HomeKept. The app starts with a 7-day free trial and no sign-up. An account (email address) is only created if you choose to subscribe, so that your subscription stays with you if you change phones. The app shows no advertising. It does send anonymous usage statistics, described below.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not show advertising, and we do not collect the Android advertising ID: the app does not request the advertising-ID permission, and advertising-ID collection and ad-personalisation signals are switched off in our analytics configuration.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and usage statistics are not used for advertising.
  • We do not read the contents of your home profile or spending — it is not transmitted to us in the first place.

Notifications

HomeKept schedules reminders locally on your device for tasks that are coming due. These are generated on the phone itself; no push tokens or reminder contents are sent to a server. You can turn notifications off in your Android settings at any time and the rest of the app keeps working.

Camera and photos

The appliance and warranty vault lets you attach a photo to an item. The camera is used only when you tap "Take photo", and Android asks for your permission first; you can also pick an existing photo, which needs no permission. The photo is copied into the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded. Files you export (CSV) go only where you choose to send them through Android's share sheet.

Usage statistics (analytics)

HomeKept uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used. The identifier involved is an app-instance ID created by Firebase for this installation; it is reset if you reinstall the app or clear its data, and we do not link it to your account or email address. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Analytics data is retained for 2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting, and is then deleted automatically.

How long we keep it

If you have an account, its record is kept for as long as the account exists. If you delete your account, the account record and its email address are removed from our authentication provider. Records of purchases are retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies, because we are required to keep proof of transactions. Data held on your device is removed when you uninstall the app, or immediately if you use "Reset all data" in Settings.

Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account at any time — see Delete your HomeKept account. You can also clear everything held on your phone yourself, without deleting your account, via Settings → Reset all data in the app.

Children

HomeKept is intended for adults maintaining a home and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Processors we use

  • Supabase — authentication (stores your email and password hash).
  • RevenueCat — subscription status.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics.
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing. We never see your card details; billing is handled entirely by Google.
