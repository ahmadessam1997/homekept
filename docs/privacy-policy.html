Privacy Policy — HomeKept

Last updated: 28 July 2026

HomeKept ("the app", package com.ahmadessam.homekept) is developed by Ahmad Essam. This policy explains exactly what the app handles.

Your home data never leaves your phone. Your home profile, your task schedule, your completion history and every cost you record are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared. The only personal data that leaves your device is your email address, used to sign in and to attach your subscription to you.

What we collect

DataWhyWho handles it
Email address To create your account, sign you in, and keep your subscription with you if you change phones. Supabase (authentication provider, acting as our processor)
Password To sign you in. Stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider — we never see or store your password. Supabase
Google account email and basic profile Only if you choose "Continue with Google" instead of a password. Google, then Supabase
Purchase and subscription status To know whether your subscription is active, and to restore it if you reinstall. Google Play and RevenueCat (billing processor)
Home profile, tasks, history, costs To build and keep your maintenance schedule. Nobody — this stays on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not read the contents of your home profile or spending — it is not transmitted to us in the first place.

Notifications

HomeKept schedules reminders locally on your device for tasks that are coming due. These are generated on the phone itself; no push tokens or reminder contents are sent to a server. You can turn notifications off in your Android settings at any time and the rest of the app keeps working.

How long we keep it

Your account record is kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the account record and its email address are removed from our authentication provider. Records of purchases are retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies, because we are required to keep proof of transactions. Data held on your device is removed when you uninstall the app, or immediately if you use "Reset all data" in Settings.

Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account at any time — see Delete your HomeKept account. You can also clear everything held on your phone yourself, without deleting your account, via Settings → Reset all data in the app.

Children

HomeKept is intended for adults maintaining a home and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Processors we use

  • Supabase — authentication (stores your email and password hash).
  • RevenueCat — subscription status.
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing. We never see your card details; billing is handled entirely by Google.
