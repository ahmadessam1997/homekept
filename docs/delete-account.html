Delete your HomeKept account

HomeKept — com.ahmadessam.homekept — developed by Ahmad Essam

You can ask us to delete your HomeKept account and the personal data attached to it at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to do this.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address you signed up with, so we can confirm the account is yours. Put "Delete my account" in the subject line.

Email a deletion request

If you signed in with Google, email us from that same Google address.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account record and email address Permanently deleted from our authentication provider within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner.
Your password hash Deleted with the account record.
Home profile, schedule, history, costs and the appliance and warranty vault (including photos) These are stored only on your phone and were never sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the app, or immediately if you tap Settings → Reset all data in the app.
Usage statistics (analytics) Held by Google Analytics for Firebase against an app-instance ID that is not linked to your account or email address, so they cannot be looked up from an account. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, discards that ID; events already collected are deleted automatically after the retention period (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting).
Purchase records Retained by Google Play and RevenueCat as proof of transaction, as we are required to keep these for tax and refund purposes. They are not used to identify you in the app after deletion.

About your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a paid subscription, because the subscription is held by Google Play rather than by us. To stop future charges, cancel it in the Play Store: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → HomeKept → Cancel. We recommend cancelling before requesting deletion.
