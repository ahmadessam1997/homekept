Delete your HomeKept account

HomeKept — com.ahmadessam.homekept — developed by Ahmad Essam

You can delete your HomeKept account and the personal data attached to it at any time, on both the Android version (Google Play) and the iPhone version (App Store). You can do it from inside the app, or ask us by email — you do not need to have the app installed to use the email route.

How to request deletion

Inside the app (Android and iPhone): open Settings → Delete account while signed in and confirm. Your account and its cloud record are deleted immediately, and you are signed out. Your home data on the phone is not affected.

By email: email us from the address you signed up with, so we can confirm the account is yours. Put "Delete my account" in the subject line.

Email a deletion request

If you signed in with Google, email us from that same Google address. If you used Sign in with Apple on iPhone, the account is registered under the email Apple gave us — your Apple ID email, or an Apple private relay address if you chose "Hide My Email"; in that case the in-app route above is the simplest, otherwise tell us which Apple ID you used.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account record and email address (including the email and optional name provided by Google sign-in or Sign in with Apple) Permanently deleted from our authentication provider immediately if you use Settings → Delete account in the app, or within 30 days of an emailed request, usually much sooner.
Your password hash Deleted with the account record.
Home profile, schedule, history, costs and the appliance and warranty vault (including photos) These are stored only on your phone and were never sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the app, or immediately if you tap Settings → Reset all data in the app.
Usage statistics (analytics) Held by Google Analytics for Firebase against an app-instance ID that is not linked to your account or email address, so they cannot be looked up from an account. Uninstalling the app (on Android or iPhone), or clearing its data in Android's settings, discards that ID; events already collected are deleted automatically after the retention period (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting).
Purchase records Retained by Google Play (Android) or Apple / the App Store (iPhone) and by RevenueCat as proof of transaction, as we are required to keep these for tax and refund purposes. They are not used to identify you in the app after deletion.

About your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a paid subscription, because the subscription is held by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone) rather than by us. To stop future charges, cancel it in the store you bought it from. On Android, in the Play Store: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → HomeKept → Cancel. On iPhone, in iOS Settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → HomeKept → Cancel Subscription (HomeKept Plus is an App Store auto-renewable subscription managed by Apple). We recommend cancelling before requesting deletion.
